How to use this app on your phone with zero apps required:

1
Add to Home Screen (PWA)

Tap your browser's menu (3 dots or share icon) and select "Add to Home screen". This app will sit on your phone screen with an app icon!

2
Create Your FastFill Bookmark

Bookmark any page in Chrome/Safari, edit it, name it ⚡ TTD FastFill, and paste the code from the Copy FastFill button above.

3
Book in 0.2 Seconds

On booking day, open TTD. When you reach the pilgrim details screen, tap your bookmark and all devotees and address fill automatically!
