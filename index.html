1. How to Setup the Chrome Extension (Desktop)

  1. Open your browser and navigate to chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions).
  2. Enable Developer mode (toggle at top-right).
  3. Click Load unpacked (top-left).
  4. Select the folder: c:\Users\Gagan K S\Documents\TTD BOt\extension.
  5. Click the TTD FastFill extension icon in your browser toolbar to pre-add your devotees and test the toggles!

2. How to Use the Mobile Web App on Your Phone (Instant PWA)

  1. Open mobile-app/index.html on your phone's Chrome or Safari browser.
  2. Tap the browser menu (three dots or share button) and select "Add to Home screen" / "Install App".
  3. The app will install directly on your phone with the official TTD icon and launch full-screen!
  4. Pre-fill your devotees, toggle who is "Going", and tap ⚡ 1-Click FastFill when booking starts.

3. How to Build the Native Android APK (Android Studio)

  1. Open Android Studio.
  2. Select Open an Existing Project and choose the folder: c:\Users\Gagan K S\Documents\TTD BOt\android.
  3. Wait for Gradle sync to complete (SDK 34, AndroidX).
  4. Click Build > Build Bundle(s) / APK(s) > Build APK(s).
  5. Android Studio will generate app-debug.apk. Transfer it to your phone and install!
  6. The app provides a dedicated full-screen browser with a bottom ⚡ 1-Click FastFill bar that fills devotee forms in under 500ms!

4. How to Use the 1-Click Phone Bookmarklet (No Install)

  1. Open the Mobile Bookmarklet Generator.
  2. Add your devotees and toggle "Going" for who will travel this time.
  3. Click Generate & Copy Mobile Bookmarklet.
  4. On your phone, bookmark any page in Chrome or Safari, edit it, rename it ⚡ TTD FastFill, and paste the code into the URL.
  5. When booking on your phone, open your bookmarks and tap it!
